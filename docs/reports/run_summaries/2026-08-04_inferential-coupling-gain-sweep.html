Each metric over the run. Thin lines are individual seeds; the thick line is their mean. When the thin lines fan apart, the mean is not a result — read the spread first. Final-value means ± sd are in the table below.

What is under test

The deployed Kuramoto term averages the other three legs UNIFORMLY (c/(n-1)). That divisor is itself a hand-set precision: every leg trusted equally, forever, which is what makes an otherwise legitimate innate reflex a script rather than inference. Each arm replaces it with a precision-weighted mean, w_j = (amp_j/(tle_j+eps))^k, so a leg pulls toward its neighbours in proportion to how well each of THEM predicts itself. k=0 is the legacy uniform mean and is byte-identical to the base; k=-1 is the wrong-sign control. Base is the nolearn2 arm (all controller learning off), because there the precision weighting is the ONLY inferential thing left in the controller. Question: does inter-leg coordination (plv_w) improve? Watch cw_spr to confirm the weights actually became unequal — it scales 0.00/0.14/0.28/0.60 with |k|, so the lever demonstrably fired.
