What is under test

Does the homeokinetic controller coordinate the legs on its own, and what does raising its learning rate to the Playful Machine's value cost? The deployed arm carries the full hand-built scaffold stack (power stroke, Kuramoto coupling toward a specified trot, postural reflex, height homeostat, heading PD, stance-lift). The two pure-HK arms carry NONE of it — no stroke, no coupling, no gait phase, no heading, no nav — so every leg motion is the learned controller. They differ from each other by ctrl_lr alone: 0.01 (ours, historically) versus 0.10 (PM's hexapod value). Inter-leg coherence had never been measured on any pure-HK arm before this run: its phase estimator was gated on a scaffold consumer being active, so stripping the scaffolds silently stripped the instrument and it returned a structural 0.000.
