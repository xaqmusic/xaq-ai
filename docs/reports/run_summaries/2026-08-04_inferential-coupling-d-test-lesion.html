What is under test

THE (d) PERTURBATION TEST. At tick 2500 leg 0's commanded motion is set to zero and left there; the world is unchanged and nothing tells the brain, so it can only notice through its own prediction error. Arms differ only in couple_prec_gain (k), which replaces the Kuramoto term's UNIFORM neighbour average with one weighted by w=(amp/(tle+eps))^k. k=0 is the legacy uniform mean; k=-1 is the wrong-sign control and is the arm that matters most, because it carries a comparable weight SPREAD (cw_spr 0.92 vs 0.98) and so tests whether the direction of the weighting matters or merely its unevenness. Read plv_w ONLY beside plv_wn: after the lesion the control loses support (0.669) while k=+2 holds it (0.914), and most of the raw plv_w gap is that support difference -- i.e. the effect is that the surviving legs KEEP OSCILLATING, not that they phase-lock more tightly.
